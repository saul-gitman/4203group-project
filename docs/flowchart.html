Presenting it in one minute

  1. Every second the aircraft gets a GNSS epoch, an IMU reading and a radar scan.
  2. Flow 1: remove the ionosphere with Klobuchar, solve SPP by least squares, and get σ_h from HDOP. Doppler least squares gives velocity, which is blended with the IMU.
  3. The no-entry zone is a GeoJSON polygon. Its margin is aircraft size plus k·σ_h, so worse satellite geometry means a wider margin.
  4. Flow 2: the radar reading is rotated from sensor to body to north/east. Its distance is measured directly, so GNSS error does not enter.
  5. For each threat: room left, reaction distance, braking and turning distance. The most urgent threat picks CLEAR, BRAKE, TURN or RETURN.
  6. Result: 4 scripted flights and 90 test runs up to 25 m/s, with no breach of the zone or the building.
